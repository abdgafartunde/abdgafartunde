
## Abd'gafar Tunde Tiamiyu

Postdoctoral Researcher in Computational and Applied Mathematics · Jilin University

I work on mathematical and learning-based methods for **inverse problems** — recovering hidden structure from noisy, incomplete measurements. Applications span subsurface imaging, medical diagnostics, and engineering simulation.

**Research:** inverse problems & regularisation · computational imaging (EIT, CT, MRI, ultrasound, seismic FWI) · physics-informed neural networks · numerical methods for PDEs

---

**Currently building**

- [`eit-reconstruction-toolkit`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/eit-reconstruction-toolkit) — EIT reconstruction: classical regularisation + deep learning
- [`seismic-inversion-ml`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/seismic-inversion-ml) — physics-informed seismic inversion for subsurface imaging
- [`medical-imaging`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/medical-imaging) — compressed sensing for accelerated MRI and CT
- [`groundwater-detection`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/groundwater-detection) — ERT inversion for groundwater mapping
