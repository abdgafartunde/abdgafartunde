## Abd'gafar Tunde Tiamiyu

Postdoctoral Researcher in Computational and Applied Mathematics · Jilin University

I work on mathematical and learning-based methods for **inverse problems**: recovering hidden structure from noisy, incomplete measurements. Applications span subsurface imaging, medical diagnostics, and engineering simulation.

**Research:** inverse problems & regularisation · computational imaging (EIT, CT, MRI, ultrasound, seismic FWI) · physics-informed neural networks · numerical methods for PDEs

---

**Featured**

- [`eit-reconstruction-toolkit`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/eit-reconstruction-toolkit): 2-D EIT reconstruction with P1 FEM, adjoint Jacobian, and Tikhonov + TV/ADMM · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21037312)
- [`medical-imaging`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/medical-imaging): compressed sensing for accelerated MRI and sparse-view CT · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21321199)
- [`image-restoration-toolkit`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/image-restoration-toolkit): adaptive and quasi-Newton first-order methods (AdGD, inertial quasi-Newton FBS, PDAL/VarPDAL) for image denoising and deblurring · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23363421)
- [`ertkit`](https://github.com/abdgafartunde/ertkit): vertical electrical sounding inversion for groundwater exploration, with depth of investigation and uncertainty (2.5-D ERT to follow) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23385097)
